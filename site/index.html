Setup

One command. That's it.

It creates your profile repo if needed, stores a token, lets you pick the cards and the look, and starts the first run.

❯ bash <(curl -fsSL https://pedrocatalao.github.io/github-cool-readme/setup.sh)
1Run itNeeds the GitHub CLI, logged in, and git.
2Pick your lookThe cards, the style, the colour and the background.
3DoneThe cards are in your README and update every 2 hours.

It asks before changing anything; add --dry-run to only see what it would do. Run it again to change the settings, or set it up by hand.

Styles

Four styles. Seven colours.

Every card in every style and colour, on a transparent background or a panel of your own colour.

GitHub's own fonts, colours and icons. The default. style: classic

Round LEDs on a grid, like an old scoreboard. style: dotmatrix

Square pixels, in any colour. style: oled

The dot matrix's round LEDs, without the grid. style: floating

Cards

Your year, on seven cards.

Private and organization work is counted, and can be turned off. Only totals appear; private repo names never do.

SummaryCommits, pull requests created and reviewed, issues and stars, for the last 12 months and all time.
LanguagesEach repo's language mix, weighted by your commits there.
Weekly rhythmYour contributions per weekday, with your busiest and quietest day.
Notable contributionsYour pull requests to other people's public projects, by stars.
Streaks optionalCurrent and longest streak, active days, best day and month.
About optionalA headline, a few lines and facts of your own.
Top repos optionalYour public repos with the most stars, and the stars each got this year.

Every setting is in the README.
